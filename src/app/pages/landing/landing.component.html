07 — Pathway guidance

Readiness or accreditation? Ask before you commit.

Most institutions are not sure which route fits their current capacity. Readiness builds the policies and systems you are missing; accreditation is the long-term status that follows. Tell us where you stand and we will tell you which one to pursue first.

  • A confidential read on your eligibility for either pathway
  • A clear comparison of cost, timeline and institutional effort
  • An honest answer on whether you are ready for accreditation yet
Email our advisory team → support@avera.capital · reply within 2 business days
Pathway A 12–36 months

GCF Readiness

A targeted grant to close fiduciary, ESS and gender gaps, then prepare a fundable project pipeline.

  • For unaccredited national, private or civil society entities
  • Typical grant size $300k–$1M+
  • Fastest route to a first GCF-funded engagement
Pathway B 24–48 months

Full accreditation

Direct Access Entity status: your institution manages GCF funds and larger investment projects itself.

  • Requires board-approved policies and clean external audits
  • Undergoes the FMCA and full accreditation review
  • Usually sequenced after a Readiness grant

Not sure which applies? Send one paragraph about your organization and we will map both routes for you.
